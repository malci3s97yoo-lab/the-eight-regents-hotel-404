01 / INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Hotel 404

An old hotel in Brazil, a suspiciously cheap place to live, and inhabitants that ordinary humans cannot even see.

The Eight Regents Hotel 404 is a supernatural setting built around death, identity, emotional energy, hierarchy, and the strange ways a person's past can shape what they become.

The building houses entities that originated from dead humans. Some control entire floors. Others are trapped there, living in the shadow of the beings who came before them.

IMPORTANT: The protagonist represents the reader, the creator, and the audience. Their identity is intentionally open.
02 / THE BUILDING

The Hotel

Founded in 1867, the hotel was originally a luxury establishment for nobles and wealthy visitors. It operated for approximately seven years before closing in 1874, following crimes and disappearances.

The building was eventually abandoned. Decades later, its doors reopened. Today, it attracts residents with incredibly low rent. The owner knows exactly what kind of place this is.

The building

Eight floors

Each floor is associated with a Regent and has its own atmosphere and influence on the entities living there.

The residents

Cheap rent

Humans are drawn in by prices that seem too good to be true. Their emotional energy becomes a resource.

The owner

Someone knows

The owner can see entities and knowingly brings new residents into the hotel. Their identity and motives remain unknown.

The hotel's history

1867

The hotel is founded in Brazil. Its builder remains unknown.

1867–1874

The hotel operates. During this period, several future Regents die within its walls.

1874

The hotel closes after crimes and disappearances.

After the closure

Akuya dies in his office on the eighth floor as authorities approach. The hotel remains abandoned.

Decades later

The future Regents awaken as entities, approximately eighty years after their individual deaths.

Present day

The hotel reopens and begins receiving new residents.

03 / THE WORLD

How this world works

Superior entities / Regents

Regents were the first entities to emerge on their respective floors. All of them were human during their lives, and their deaths are connected to Akuya Scarlet Luther.

They are bound to the floors where they died. They possess more accumulated energy than lower entities and can feed on both human emotional energy and other entities.

Lower entities

Lower entities are souls of humans who died inside the hotel. Many were victims of the Regents, but not every person who dies returns as an entity.

Strong emotions at the moment of death may influence whether a soul remains: anger, fear, resentment, betrayal, despair, or a powerful feeling of injustice.

THE LOWER ENTITIES WILL BE INTRODUCED GRADUALLY. This is an ongoing world. New residents and their stories will be added as they are revealed. The current list is not the complete population of the hotel.

Energy and survival

Entities feed on emotional energy from human residents. Regents can also consume lower entities. As humans lose emotional energy, they may become increasingly grey, apathetic, exhausted, and disconnected from their feelings.

Names and souls

An entity's true name can be used to interfere with its soul. Depending on the circumstances and intent, this may lead to liberation or destruction. For this reason, true names are kept secret.

Manifestation and appearance

An entity's appearance can be influenced by three main things:

Death

The circumstances of death may leave physical or symbolic marks.

Subconscious

Memories, fears, desires, and accumulated thoughts can shape an entity's form.

Personal influence

Interests, personality, and the floor itself can influence the final appearance.

Territories and movement

Lower entities generally remain on the floors where they belong. This is not an absolute physical restriction, but a matter of survival: entering another floor means stepping into territory controlled by a different Regent and its inhabitants.

A lower entity can travel to another floor when accompanied by its own superior entity. The Regent's presence offers protection and makes it possible for its followers to move through unfamiliar territory.

For example, Kittyn and CerRui can leave the fourth floor when accompanying Squigscar. The same principle applies to the other Regents and the lower entities who follow them.

TERRITORY MATTERS. A lower entity wandering alone into another Regent's floor may be placing itself in a dangerous situation.

General rules

  • The hotel has exactly eight floors. A basement has not been decided.
  • The building is not sentient and does not change its architecture.
  • Humans can enter and leave unless an entity intervenes.
  • Ordinary humans cannot see entities. The protagonist and owner are exceptions.
  • Regents are trapped inside the hotel.
  • Lower entities usually remain on their respective floors for survival, but can travel with their superior entity.
  • Entities cannot die a second time.
  • Floors influence the appearance and themes of their inhabitants.
  • The hotel is not physically a prison. Its inhabitants are the danger.

05 / THE REGENTS

The Superior Entities

Seven active Regents are currently known. The sixth floor is vacant.

01. Wincorter

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 1

Wincorter was a young, handsome, mute man who worked at the hotel. He carried luggage, helped guests, and assisted with the elevator. He communicated through gestures and often smiled politely because people did not understand him.

He disliked his own face because it resembled his father's. He also disliked his smile, which he felt was false. Reserved and uncomfortable with loud sounds, he rarely complained.

Now he demands absolute silence on the first floor. His face has been replaced by a dark, melting mass. Four tentacle-like appendages end in small snake heads. Each head can speak part of a sentence, allowing them to communicate together.

The lower entities of this floor often resemble hotel staff: servants, butlers, porters, bellhops, and employees.

Known as: Wincorter.

True name: Unknown.

02. Knowless

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 2

A former scientist who was promising for his time. Calm and curious, he is fascinated by human anatomy and struggles to restrain his desire to investigate living bodies.

His current form has the upper part of his head missing. A dark tentacle-like structure emerges from it. One arm is replaced by a dark appendage capable of forming tools and instruments.

He can be clumsy, sometimes knocking objects over because humans cannot see entities. He also struggles with modern technology and tends to rely on outdated theories.

His lower entities often have scientific, anatomical, or assistant-related traits. Some help him with his studies or try to calm him down.

Known as: Knowless.

True name: Unknown.

03. Goldpride

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 3

Goldpride was once Duoei, a wealthy boy who grew up surrounded by privilege and strict expectations. His mother was controlling, miserly, and obsessed with social class.

He is vain, arrogant, controlling, and cruel. He is obsessed with appearance and social position. He cannot stand being outshone and may disfigure those he considers more beautiful.

His floor reflects aristocracy, luxury, hierarchy, and vanity. Lower entities are expected to look immaculate and remain socially inferior to him.

Duoei and Damien were childhood friends. On Damien's birthday, Duoei ran away with an elegant peacock-shaped toy to give him a present. Later, he witnessed Akuya and Damien killing Dolita. Akuya pressured Damien into killing his friend.

Duoei was buried with the toy. After awakening, he became increasingly cruel. His current design retains dark aristocratic aesthetics, peacock-inspired visual elements, and details connected to his death.

He still calls Squigscar "unicorn", a childhood nickname. Squigscar usually ignores him, which only makes him more irritated.

Goldpride is accompanied by a group of four masked female lower entities. They serve as his personal attendants and remain close to him as he moves through the hotel.

One of these attendants is GoldRat, who walks on Goldpride's right side carrying a candle. Another wears a bird mask and walks on his left, also carrying a candle. The central attendant carries a fan and uses it to cool Goldpride.

Known as: Goldpride.

True name: Duoei (surname unknown).

04. Squigscar

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 4 / ROOM 404

Squigscar is a strange, unpredictable entity with a childlike curiosity. He is fascinated by small things, sometimes eats objects, and can be calm even with his monstrous appearance.

His form resembles a distorted horse or unicorn. He has one horn, a long neck, pointed teeth, unusual limbs, and a rope around his neck. He can stretch his neck and limbs, detach his head, and transform into a more terrifying form.

In life, Damien was Akuya's younger brother. Their parents favored Damien, while Akuya was bullied and repressed. Akuya encouraged Damien's unusual behavior and became the person Damien most wanted approval from.

As they grew older, Akuya's influence became increasingly dangerous. Damien became involved in the hotel's crimes, including the death of Duoei. Eventually he tried to leave the hotel and escape his brother's control. Akuya stopped him and killed him.

After awakening, Damien gradually became Squigscar. He began observing animals and strange objects, then eventually started targeting people who reminded him of his past or of Akuya.

Squigscar is fascinated by the protagonist's heterochromia: one green eye and one black eye. He calls the protagonist his pet and wants them to become the Regent of floor six.

His favorite lower entities are Kittyn and CerRui. He also has a complicated relationship with Goldpride and a distant, painful connection to Akuya.

Known as: Squigscar.

True name: Damien Scarlet Luther.

Height: 2.58 m.

05. Meathew

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 5

Meathew was a talented chef who came to the hotel looking for accommodation. He was interested in unusual culinary ideas, but preferred cooking and refining dishes over committing murder.

He died one day after arriving at the hotel, after being attacked by Akuya. Damien was also involved in the events surrounding his death.

His current appearance includes a missing eye, an apron with a knife engraved over the eye area, and dark features connected to the hotel's violence.

After awakening, his culinary curiosity became an obsession. He uses lower entities as ingredients and forces victims to participate in his disturbing meals.

The fifth floor is populated by entities with traits connected to cooks, butchers, kitchen helpers, utensils, ingredients, and food preparation.

Meathew wants the protagonist, but Squigscar has forbidden him from taking them.

Known as: Meathew.

True name: Unknown.

06. The Empty Floor / Geraldo

VACANT FLOOR / FORMER REGENT

The sixth floor once belonged to Geraldo, a Regent who was exorcised by humans. His disappearance changed how the other Regents think about their own existence.

His case made the others more careful with their true names. A name may be used to interfere with a soul, and the possibility of being destroyed or liberated is frightening to them.

Squigscar wants the protagonist to take over the sixth floor. The protagonist refuses.

The exact circumstances of Geraldo's exorcism and the process through which a human could become a Regent remain undecided.

Former Regent: Geraldo.

Current status: Vacant.

07. Dolita

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 7

Dolita was six years old when she died. In life, she was a sweet child who loved porcelain dolls and cloth toys.

She was killed by Akuya and Damien during the hotel's operating period. Duoei witnessed the event.

After awakening, Dolita retained a childlike personality, but became extremely cruel. Over time, she developed an obsession with collecting dolls.

Her current form resembles a supernatural doll. A rabbit plush is an important part of her appearance and abilities. She can use toys to manipulate and torment other entities.

The seventh floor resembles an island of dolls. Its inhabitants may become cloth figures, with stitches, fabrics, porcelain, plush details, and doll-like features.

Known as: Dolita.

True name: Unknown.

08. Kordiller

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 8

Kordiller is Damien's older brother and the former person responsible for the hotel. He had very red hair and was considered handsome, but was bullied and repressed by his family.

He found companionship in Damien and encouraged his unusual behavior. However, Kordiller was controlling and knew how to use Damien's desire for approval to manipulate him.

Kordiller obtained the hotel and used it for crimes. He selected visitors according to his interests, particularly people whose emotional suffering attracted his attention.

His floor carries an oppressive energy. Other Regents avoid it, and there are no known lower entities on the eighth floor. Even ordinary hotel staff avoid entering its rooms.

Kordiller died in his office as authorities approached the hotel. His current form includes rope, dark horns, reddish elements, and an infernal visual influence.

He remains isolated on the top floor, away from the other Regents.

Known as: Kordiller.

True name: Akuya Scarlet Luther.

06 / THE RESIDENTS

Other inhabitants

Not every important character in the hotel is a Regent. Some of the most memorable inhabitants are lower entities, humans, or figures whose origins are still unknown.

Kittyn

LOWER ENTITY / FLOOR 4

A cat-like clown with one eye positioned lower than the other and a spiral eye. The deliberately misaligned appearance gives Kittyn a strange, unsettling expression.

In life, Kittyn was a repressed individual. After awakening, death felt like a gift and an opportunity to act without the restrictions that once held them back.

Kittyn is friends with Squigscar and shares his strange activities. However, Kittyn dislikes the protagonist and wants to throw them in the trash.

Kittyn may appear protective or affectionate toward the protagonist, but this is misleading. Jealousy over Squigscar's attention is part of the problem.

Kittyn also has an ongoing rivalry with GoldRat, one of Goldpride's attendants. GoldRat frequently provokes Kittyn through gestures, taking advantage of the fact that Kittyn cannot understand what she is feeling behind her mask.

Known as: Kittyn.

True name: Not publicly disclosed.

CerRui

LOWER ENTITY / FLOOR 4

A large black dog with an eye in its throat. Because of this feature, CerRui cannot speak.

CerRui is one of Squigscar's favorite lower entities. Its personality is still being developed.

Known as: CerRui.

True name: Unknown.

GoldRat

LOWER ENTITY / FLOOR 3 / PERSONAL ATTENDANT

GoldRat is one of Goldpride's personal attendants and the principal lower entity among the group of masked women who accompany him.

Despite being referred to as a rat, GoldRat is not exactly a rat-like creature. Her most recognizable feature is a golden rat mask that completely covers her head.

She carries a candle and walks on Goldpride's right side. Her appearance is carefully styled, reflecting Goldpride's influence and the elegant aesthetic of the third floor.

GoldRat cannot speak because her entire head is replaced by a mask. She communicates through gestures and movements.

Her behavior becomes especially provocative around Kittyn. She enjoys teasing him, pulling his ears unexpectedly, pushing him around, and making fun of him through gestures. She has even been known to kick him down the stairs.

Kittyn can speak, but cannot read GoldRat's expressions because there is no visible face behind her mask. This makes their rivalry even more frustrating for him.

Goldpride's attendants

GoldRat is accompanied by three other masked women. Together, they form a group of four personal attendants who remain close to Goldpride.

  • GoldRat: Walks on Goldpride's right side, carrying a candle. Her mask resembles a golden rat.
  • The bird-masked attendant: Walks on Goldpride's left side, also carrying a candle.
  • The fan attendant: Walks in the middle, holding a fan and using it to cool Goldpride.
  • The fourth attendant: Her mask and specific function have not yet been established.

Like GoldRat, the other attendants cannot speak because their masks completely replace their heads.

Known as: GoldRat.

Role: Goldpride's principal lower entity and attendant.

True name: Unknown.

The Protagonist

HUMAN / MAIN CHARACTER

The protagonist represents the reader and intentionally has an open identity. They are a human who is short on money and rents a suspiciously cheap room in the hotel.

They can see entities, unlike ordinary humans. Their eyes have heterochromia: one green eye and one black eye.

Squigscar becomes fascinated by their eyes, calls them his pet, and wants them to become the Regent of the sixth floor. The protagonist refuses.

The Hotel Owner

IDENTITY UNKNOWN

The owner manages the hotel and attracts new residents with extremely cheap rent. They can see entities and know the truth about the building.

The owner knowingly provides new humans as a source of emotional energy. Their species, age, identity, and motives remain unknown.

MORE RESIDENTS WILL BE ADDED. The lower entities are not all documented yet. Their profiles will appear here gradually as the hotel expands.

09 / LORE

Things the hotel remembers

The legend of Geraldo

Geraldo was the former Regent of the sixth floor. Humans managed to exorcise him, proving that even a superior entity could be vulnerable.

The other Regents became more secretive about their true names. The possibility of losing their existence, or being liberated against their will, became a source of fear.

Their memories of Geraldo are inconsistent. Even Squigscar and Kordiller do not remember him in exactly the same way.

There is also a running joke: whenever someone mentions Geraldo, the other Regents instinctively apologize. Some insist that everyone should remember him.

Central themes
  • Identity and the subconscious.
  • Death and the way it influences appearance.
  • Emotional distortion and the loss of human feelings.
  • Hierarchy, control, and power.
  • How people repeat patterns from their past.
  • The difference between appearance, personality, and morality.

The entities are not simply divided into good and evil. Their histories, choices, and contradictions matter. A character can be affectionate and dangerous, calm and disturbing, or sympathetic without being innocent.
