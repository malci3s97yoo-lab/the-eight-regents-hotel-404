01 / INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Hotel 404

An old hotel in Brazil, suspiciously cheap rooms, and inhabitants ordinary humans cannot even see.

The Eight Regents Hotel 404 is a supernatural setting built around death, identity, emotional energy, hierarchy, and the strange ways a person's past can shape what they become.

The building houses entities that originated from dead humans. Some control entire floors. Others remain trapped there, living under the influence of the beings who came before them.

IMPORTANT: The protagonist represents the reader and intentionally has an open identity.
02 / THE BUILDING

The Hotel

Founded in Brazil in 1867, the hotel was originally a luxury establishment for nobles and wealthy visitors. It operated until 1874, when complaints, investigations, crimes, and disappearances led to its closure.

The building was eventually abandoned. Much later, it reopened under a new owner who attracts residents with extremely cheap rooms. The owner knows what kind of place this is and uses its residents as sources of energy.

03 / THE WORLD

How this world works

Superior entities / Regents

The Regents were human during their lives. Their deaths are connected to Akuya Scarlet Luther, and each became bound to a floor of the hotel. They possess more accumulated energy than lower entities and can feed on humans and other entities.

Regents cannot die a second time through ordinary means. Their true names are dangerous secrets because knowing a true name can allow someone to interfere directly with that entity's soul, potentially freeing or destroying it.

Lower entities

Lower entities are mostly souls of humans who died inside the hotel, often victims of the Regents. Not every victim awakens. Powerful emotions at the moment of death, such as rage, fear, resentment, betrayal, despair, or a sense of injustice, may cause a person to remain as an entity.

Lower entities are conscious, but their abilities and limitations vary. Some can speak, others cannot. They are generally tied to the floor where they died and are often influenced by its Regent, but they do not automatically obey that Regent.

THE LOWER ENTITIES WILL BE INTRODUCED GRADUALLY.

This is an ongoing world. New residents and their stories will be added as they are revealed. The current list is not the complete population of the hotel.

Energy and survival

Entities feed on human vital and emotional energy. Feeding can gradually cause exhaustion, apathy, emotional wear, depression, reduced motivation, and psychological deterioration. It does not necessarily require physical contact.

Some entities deliberately manipulate a person's emotional suffering and may guide a weakened victim toward a fatal outcome. This is not true of every entity. Regents can also consume lower entities, forming part of the hotel's supernatural food chain.

Physical interaction

Entities can touch, hold, restrain, and interfere with humans and objects. Most cannot directly kill humans through physical force, although exceptions exist and have not yet been identified. Entities may prevent residents from leaving, even though the building itself is not an architectural prison.

Appearance and manifestation

An entity's appearance can be influenced by the circumstances of death, the human subconscious, memories, fears, desires, trauma, personality, and the floor itself. The floor influences lower entities, but they are not copies of their Regent.

Death

The circumstances of death may leave physical or symbolic marks.

Subconscious

Memories, fears, desires, and accumulated thoughts can shape an entity's form.

Personal influence

Interests, personality, and the floor itself can influence the final appearance.

Territories and movement

Lower entities are generally tied to the floors where they belong. They can travel to another floor when accompanied by their own superior entity. The Regent's presence offers protection in unfamiliar territory. For example, Kittyn and CerRui can leave the fourth floor when accompanying Squigscar.

Humans and the hotel

Ordinary humans cannot see entities. The protagonist initially could not see them either, but this changed after the events involving Squigscar. The current owner is another known exception.

The hotel has a fixed architecture, exactly eight floors, and no consciousness of its own. Humans can enter and leave unless an entity physically intervenes. Regents are bound to the building, while lower entities are tied to their respective floors.

THE BUILDING IS NOT THE PRISON. Its inhabitants are the ones who can turn a stay into captivity.

General rules

  • The hotel has exactly eight floors. A basement has not been established.
  • The building is not sentient and does not change its architecture.
  • Ordinary humans cannot see entities. The protagonist and owner are exceptions.
  • Regents are bound to the hotel; lower entities are tied to their floors.
  • Lower entities may travel with their own superior entity.
  • Most entities cannot directly kill humans physically, but exceptions exist.
  • Entities can physically restrain humans and interfere with their departure.
  • Entities feed on human energy; Regents can also consume lower entities.
  • True names can interfere with an entity's soul.

05 / THE REGENTS

The Superior Entities

Seven active Regents are currently known. The sixth floor is vacant.

01. Wincorter

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 1

Wincorter was a young, handsome, mute man who worked at the hotel. He carried luggage, helped guests, and assisted with the elevator. He communicated through gestures and often smiled politely because people did not understand him.

He disliked his own face because it resembled his father's. He also disliked his smile, which he felt was false. Reserved and uncomfortable with loud sounds, he rarely complained.

Now he demands absolute silence on the first floor. His supernatural form includes a dark mass covering part of his face and four tentacle-like extensions ending in small snake heads. The heads can manipulate objects and speak in fragmented phrases. He still uses gestures.

He was killed by the brothers during the hotel's operation and resents Akuya and Damien. His lower entities are associated with porters, servants, and hotel staff. They cannot make sound on his floor.

Known as: Wincorter. True name: Unknown.

02. Knowless

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 2

Knowless was a scientist with a calm temperament and an intense curiosity about human anatomy and the interior of the body. He is not naturally cruel or cold, but his curiosity can become obsessive. He tries to control his impulses and sometimes fails.

His current form has part of the upper head missing, with a dark tentacle or horn emerging from the opening. One arm is absent and replaced by a dark tentacle that can take the shape of instruments. His clothing is torn. He is clumsy and may knock objects over because humans cannot see him.

In life, he was a promising scientist whose beliefs were shaped by the knowledge of his time. Akuya and Damien killed him with axes. After awakening, he retained his scientific curiosity. He struggles with modern technology and may misunderstand phones or contemporary medical equipment, despite being intelligent in his specialty.

Knowless eventually forgave Squigscar. His lower entities often have scientific, anatomical, or assistant-related traits.

Known as: Knowless. True name: Unknown.

03. Goldpride

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 3

Goldpride was once Duoei, a wealthy boy raised among privilege and strict expectations. His mother was controlling, miserly, and obsessed with social class. After awakening, Duoei repeated many of the traits he had grown up around.

He is vain, arrogant, controlling, and cruel. He is obsessed with appearance and social position, cannot stand being outshone, and may disfigure those he considers more beautiful. His floor reflects aristocracy, luxury, hierarchy, and vanity. Lower entities are expected to look immaculate and remain socially inferior to him.

Duoei and Damien were childhood best friends. Damien had unusual habits from a young age, and Duoei was one of the few people who managed to get close to him. On Damien's birthday, Duoei ran away from home to give him a peacock made of porcelain.

Later, Duoei arrived at the hotel and witnessed Akuya and Damien involved in Dolita's death. Akuya noticed him, emotionally manipulated Damien, and pressured him into participating in the killing of his friend. Damien repeatedly struck Duoei. Duoei's body was later buried alongside the porcelain peacock.

After awakening, Duoei became increasingly cruel and began killing people and turning some of them into lower entities of his floor. His current form has visible cracks and retains a dark aristocratic aesthetic with peacock-inspired elements.

VISUAL DISTINCTION: Duoei's design is not based on a doll, doll joints, or stitched porcelain-doll features. Those visual themes belong primarily to Dolita. The porcelain peacock is a symbol of Duoei's bond with Damien and part of his human history.

He still calls Squigscar "unicorn", a childhood nickname also used by Akuya. Squigscar usually ignores him, which only makes him more irritated. Their former friendship has become resentment, provocation, and unresolved conflict.

Goldpride is accompanied by four masked female lower entities who serve as his personal attendants. GoldRat walks on his right side carrying a candle. A bird-masked attendant walks on his left, also carrying a candle. A central attendant carries a fan and uses it to cool Goldpride. The fourth attendant's specific role remains undefined.

Known as: Goldpride. True name: Duoei (surname unknown).

04. Squigscar

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 4 / ROOM 404

Squigscar is unpredictable, peculiar, possessive, invasive, and morally detached. He has unusual interests, childhood habits, and a fascination with animals and objects not meant to be eaten. He understands human social rules and discomfort, but may choose to ignore them. He is not simply cute, innocent, shy, or harmless.

His form resembles a distorted horse or unicorn. He has one horn, a very long neck, pointed teeth including human-like teeth, unusual pointed limbs, and a rope around his neck. He can stretch his neck, fingers, and limbs, detach his head, remain as a head, and transform into a more terrifying appearance.

His life as Damien

Damien Scarlet Luther was Akuya's younger brother. Their parents favored Damien, while Akuya was bullied and repressed because of his red hair and appearance. Akuya found companionship in Damien and encouraged his unusual behavior. Damien licked stones, ate plants, and experimented on animals. Their parents eventually separated them when Akuya was around seventeen and Damien around eight. Akuya was expelled, while Damien remained at home.

Akuya later returned, found Damien locked in a closet, and invited him to flee. Damien did not know that Akuya had killed their parents. Years later, Akuya gained control of the hotel and brought Damien there as a miniature manager, calling him his "mini gerente unicórnio". Akuya gradually pressured Damien into violent crimes. Damien was initially uncomfortable but yielded under emotional manipulation and dependence.

Duoei's death was a turning point. Duoei was one of the few people who offered Damien a connection outside Akuya's violence. Damien killed him under Akuya's manipulation.

Damien eventually planned to escape with money and report the crimes. Akuya discovered the plan. Damien pretended to apologize, but when Akuya demanded proof of loyalty involving Damien's own life, Damien could not follow through and fled. Akuya caught him, removed his legs, and killed him with a fatal blow to the head using a pointed stone. His body was taken to room 404.

Damien awakened approximately eighty years later. At first, he wandered the hotel, observed rats, poked small animals, and repeated childhood habits. Over time, he began attacking violent or aggressive people and those he associated with his former self or Akuya.

Relationship with the protagonist

The protagonist accidentally asked Squigscar to marry them while carrying out unspecified illegal activities. Squigscar accepted and now considers the protagonist his legitimate husband. There is no official marriage, and the protagonist does not necessarily share this interpretation.

Before the protagonist gained spiritual sight, Squigscar made supernatural manifestations, including licking. Other actions from that period remain unknown. Once the protagonist could see him, Squigscar was delighted, while the protagonist was frightened. The protagonist can now interrupt his manifestations and confront him.

Squigscar may smile, answer in peculiar ways, recognize serious discomfort, or attempt to make amends. This does not turn him into a gentle or moral character. If the protagonist wants to leave, he may first try to restore closeness and may eventually use his supernatural authority to keep them inside.

Favorite lower entities: Kittyn and CerRui. True name: Damien Scarlet Luther. Height: 3.58 m.

05. Meathew

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 5

Meathew was a talented chef with an intense interest in culinary experimentation. He was curious about cooking with human meat but did not initially want to become a murderer. He was independent and disliked having assistants.

He arrived at the hotel and died after only one day. Akuya attacked him, and Meathew fought back. Damien was involved. Meathew was killed using the hotel's stove, which influenced his supernatural appearance.

His current form has one eye missing, an apron of knives engraved with an eye, and a dark tentacle or horn emerging from his head. After awakening, his culinary curiosity became an obsession. He uses lower entities as ingredients and may force them to eat his dishes. His aura can compel lower entities to participate.

He is interested in the protagonist's flesh, but Squigscar prevents him from acting on that interest. Squigscar considers Meathew an excellent cook and says he might have been a fan if they had met under different circumstances.

His lower entities are associated with cooks, butchers, kitchen assistants, ingredients, and utensils. True name: Unknown.

06. Geraldo / Vacant floor

FORMER REGENT / FLOOR 6 / EXORCISED

The sixth floor is currently empty. Geraldo was its former Regent and was exorcised by humans. His removal changed how the other Regents treat the secrecy of their true names.

When Geraldo is mentioned, the other Regents may automatically ask forgiveness. Some demand that the others remember him and may slap someone who forgets. Squigscar says he would tattoo Geraldo's name on his forehead if he could. Squigscar and Akuya oddly struggle to remember him properly, while the others have inconsistent memories.

Geraldo represents the possibility that a Regent can be vulnerable and that a true name may allow a soul to be released or destroyed. Squigscar wants the protagonist to become the new Regent of the sixth floor, but the protagonist refuses.

True name: Unknown.

07. Dolita

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 7

Dolita was a six-year-old girl who loved porcelain and cloth dolls. She was a child and was not inherently cruel during her human life. Akuya and Damien killed her. Duoei witnessed her death before he was later killed himself.

Her current personality retains a childlike quality, but decades as an entity have made her extremely cruel. She collects dolls. At first she made dolls herself, but eventually that was no longer enough.

Her supernatural form is doll-like, with a bunny plush as a central object. She can manipulate victims through toys and dolls. Her floor resembles an island of dolls. Lower entities are influenced by cloth, plush, fabric, seams, stitching, and porcelain. Some are sewn to walls or incorporated into the floor's decor.

Known as: Dolita. True name: Unknown.

08. Kordiller / Akuya Scarlet Luther

ACTIVE REGENT / FLOOR 8 / FORMER HOTEL OWNER

Kordiller was Akuya Scarlet Luther, Damien's older brother and the original owner of the hotel. He was a handsome red-haired man who was bullied and repressed because of his appearance, while his parents favored Damien.

Akuya found companionship in Damien, encouraged his unusual behavior, and cultivated Damien's dependence on his approval. He was controlling and manipulative, using that dependence to coerce Damien into crimes.

Akuya used the hotel as a place for crimes and selected visitors according to his interests. He enjoyed emotional suffering and exerting control over others. When investigations and police approached in 1874, he died in the hotel office before facing justice.

His current form carries rope, dark or red horns, and a hellfire-like aesthetic. The eighth floor is oppressive. Other Regents avoid it, and very few lower entities remain there because of Akuya's overwhelming presence.

Known as: Kordiller. True name: Akuya Scarlet Luther.

06 / THE RESIDENTS

Lower Entities & Other Residents

The known residents are only a small part of the hotel's population. More entities will be added as their designs and histories are developed.

Kittyn / Félix Uruguma

LOWER ENTITY / FLOOR 4

Kittyn is a 2.15 m tall cat clown. One eye is displaced downward and the other has a spiral appearance. His anatomy is animal-like and theatrical.

In life, Félix was repressed and had psychopathic tendencies. After death, he sees his new condition as a gift because he can act more freely.

He is one of Squigscar's closest and favorite lower entities. He dislikes the protagonist and wants to throw them in the trash. His jealousy comes from Squigscar paying attention to the protagonist, not from a desire to protect them. Kittyn is irritated by Squigscar's one-sided marriage interpretation.

Despite his hostility, Kittyn likes the rice balls made by the protagonist. He may act affectionate or protective, but his intentions can be very different from what readers initially assume.

True name: Félix Uruguma. Height: 2.15 m.

CerRui

LOWER ENTITY / FLOOR 4

CerRui is a large black dog-like entity. One eye is located in his throat, affecting his ability to speak. His true name is unknown. He measures approximately 1.92 m while lying or standing on all fours.

He is one of Squigscar's favorite lower entities. His personality and full history are still being developed.

True name: Unknown. Height: approximately 1.92 m on all fours.

GoldRat

LOWER ENTITY / FLOOR 3 / PERSONAL ATTENDANT

GoldRat is one of Goldpride's personal attendants and the principal lower entity among the group of masked women who accompany him. Despite the name, she is not exactly a rat-like creature. Her most recognizable feature is a golden rat mask that completely covers her head.

She carries a candle and walks on Goldpride's right side. Her appearance is carefully styled, reflecting Goldpride's influence and the elegant aesthetic of the third floor. She cannot speak because her entire head is replaced by a mask; she communicates through gestures and movements.

Her behavior becomes especially provocative around Kittyn. She enjoys teasing him, pulling his ears unexpectedly, pushing him around, and making fun of him through gestures. She has even been known to kick him down the stairs. Kittyn struggles to read her expressions because there is no visible face behind the mask.

Goldpride's attendants

  • GoldRat: Walks on Goldpride's right side, carrying a candle. Her mask resembles a golden rat.
  • The bird-masked attendant: Walks on Goldpride's left side, also carrying a candle.
  • The fan attendant: Walks in the middle, holding a fan and using it to cool Goldpride.
  • The fourth attendant: Her mask and specific function have not yet been established.

The other attendants also cannot speak because their masks completely replace their heads. True name: Unknown.

The Protagonist

HUMAN / MAIN CHARACTER

The protagonist represents the reader and intentionally has an open identity. They are human, short on money, and rent a suspiciously cheap room in the hotel. They have heterochromia: one green eye and one black eye.

At first, they could not see entities. After an accidental marriage proposal to Squigscar, followed by supernatural events, they gained the ability to see the hotel's inhabitants. Squigscar considers the protagonist his husband, but this is his personal interpretation. There is no official or mutually agreed marriage.

The protagonist can reject unwanted contact and confront Squigscar. They refuse his wish for them to become Regent of the sixth floor.

The Hotel Owner

IDENTITY UNKNOWN

The owner manages the hotel, attracts residents with extremely cheap rent, can see entities, and knows the truth about the building. Their identity, nature, origin, relationship with the Regents, and motives remain unknown.

10 / THEMES

Central themes

Identity & subconscious

A person's history and inner world influence the form they take after death.

Emotional deformation

Death and accumulated experiences can intensify or distort existing traits.

Hierarchy

The hotel's floors reproduce social structures, power, and domination.

Repetition

Entities may repeat patterns from their human lives: Goldpride echoes his mother, Squigscar repeats childhood habits, Meathew repeats his culinary obsession, Dolita returns to dolls, and Akuya repeats control.

Dependency & feeding

Energy, emotional dependence, and unequal relationships shape survival inside the hotel.

Freedom & possession

The hotel explores the conflict between a person's autonomy and the desire of others to control or keep them.

The characters are not divided into simple categories of good and evil. Their histories, choices, contradictions, and relationships matter.
